# Coverage and release roadmap

Implemented: native offline dashboard/reader/search, 15 bilingual summaries, source dates/draft status, local checklist/notes, water estimate, portable HTML and automated preview downloads/tests.

Before a comprehensive public handbook:

- Expand every topic in BUILD_SPEC.md; present summaries are introductory.
- Review medical, water, electrical, structural, radiation and infection guidance with qualified people; review German translations and record actual review scope/dates.
- Add child/infant resuscitation, choking, anaphylaxis, seizures, diabetes, poisoning and detailed wound-care protocols, accessible diagrams and extended-disruption/recovery plans.
- Cover medical equipment, disability/accessibility, pets and combined hazards in more depth.
- Test under time pressure and on physical Android devices; establish correction/maintenance processes.
- Finish licence/dependency audit, target-SDK migration, protected production signing and store requirements.

No guaranteed survival, exhaustive information, clinical approval, live alerts, verified safe routes, pathogen identification or phone radiation measurement is claimed. iOS needs separate development and distribution-licence assessment; the APK does not install on iPhone.
